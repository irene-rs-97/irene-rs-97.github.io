# Irene Rodríguez Sánchez

I am a data professional focused on building reliable data workflows, ETL processes and analytical solutions that support decision-making and operational efficiency. My work combines Python, SQL, PySpark and Microsoft Fabric with automation and deployment tools such as n8n, Supabase and Vercel.
