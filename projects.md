# Projects

This section presents a selection of projects that illustrate my work in healthcare analytics, predictive modelling and data-driven decision support.

## Intelligent Scoring System for Hospital Discharge and Transfer Decisions
A project addressing two operational challenges in healthcare: premature discharges and avoidable readmissions. It combines a predictive model with a multicriteria decision algorithm and an analysis of hospital occupancy.

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/tfm-sns-pressure-index

## Hospital Activity Analysis (INE Data)
Exploratory and modelling work using public INE datasets to understand hospital activity across regions and time. Includes PCA, clustering, XGBoost/SHAP and time-series modelling.

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/tfm-hospital-activity-eda

## Synthetic Clinical Dataset Generator
A pipeline for generating realistic, privacy-preserving clinical datasets for experimentation and model development.

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/dataset-sintetico
