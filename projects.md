# Projects
A selection of the work I’ve developed around healthcare analytics, predictive modelling and data‑driven decision support.  
Each project reflects a mix of technical depth, practical relevance and a clear focus on improving how healthcare systems understand and manage their activity.

---

## 🏥 Intelligent Scoring System for Hospital Discharge & Transfer Decisions
A project built around two real challenges in healthcare: premature discharges and avoidable readmissions.  
I designed a predictive model and a multicriteria decision algorithm to support clinicians when hospital occupancy is high and decisions must be made quickly but safely.

**Key elements:**  
- Predictive modelling (XGBoost, SHAP)  
- Multicriteria decision logic  
- Occupancy analysis and operational insights  

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/tfm-sns-pressure-index

---

## 📊 Hospital Activity EDA (INE Data)
An exploratory and modelling‑focused analysis of Spanish hospital activity using public INE datasets.  
The goal was to uncover patterns, clusters and temporal behaviours that help explain how hospitals operate across regions and over time.

**Key elements:**  
- PCA and clustering  
- XGBoost + SHAP interpretability  
- SARIMA time‑series modelling  
- Regional and temporal comparisons  

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/tfm-hospital-activity-eda

## 🧪 Synthetic Clinical Dataset Generator
A pipeline designed to generate realistic, privacy‑preserving clinical datasets for experimentation and model training.  
Useful for testing algorithms, prototyping ideas and sharing reproducible examples without exposing sensitive data.

**Key elements:**  
- Statistical modelling  
- Distribution fitting  
- Synthetic data generation  
- Privacy‑friendly experimentation  

**Repository:** https://github.com/irene-rs-97/dataset-sintetico
